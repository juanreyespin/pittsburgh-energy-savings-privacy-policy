Pittsburgh Energy Savings

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Pittsburgh Energy Savings ("we," "us," or "our") helps homeowners in the Duquesne Light and West Penn Power service areas find out whether they qualify for an energy savings program. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you interact with our advertisements, website, or online forms, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

By submitting information through our forms, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through one of our forms (including Facebook Lead Ads forms), we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Your self-reported credit score range
  • Whether you own or rent your home
  • Your home's approximate sun/roof exposure
  • Your electric utility provider
  • General location information (such as city or county) associated with your submission
Credit score range is self-reported by you and is used only to help us gauge program eligibility. We do not run a credit check or pull your credit report, and this information does not constitute a "consumer report" under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Determine whether your home may be eligible for our energy savings program
  • Contact you by phone, text message, or email to discuss your eligibility and schedule an appointment
  • Improve and personalize our advertising and outreach
  • Maintain records of leads and appointments for our internal sales process
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Text Messages & Phone Calls

By submitting your phone number, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Pittsburgh Energy Savings and its representatives regarding your inquiry, including automated or pre-recorded messages, at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, or ask to be placed on our internal do-not-call list during any call.

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share or sell your information with the following categories of recipients:

  • Our own sales representatives and appointment setters, so they can follow up with you
  • Service providers who help us operate our business, such as scheduling, messaging, and customer relationship management platforms
  • Meta/Facebook, solely as necessary to deliver our advertisements and receive your form submission, subject to Meta's own privacy policy
  • Third-party installers, contractors, and energy program partners — we may sell or share your information with one or more third-party businesses so they can contact you about energy savings offers, whether or not you proceed past your initial inquiry
  • Law enforcement or regulators, if required by law
We may sell or transfer your contact and inquiry information to one or more third-party installers or energy service providers so they can offer you their own products or services. Any such third party is responsible for its own compliance when contacting you.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including to follow up on your inquiry, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. You may request that we delete your information at any time; see Section 7.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old and are homeowners or prospective homeowners. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may:

  • Ask us what information we have collected about you
  • Request that we correct or delete your information
  • Opt out of future contact by phone, text, or email at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 9.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
